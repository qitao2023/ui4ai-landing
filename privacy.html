Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

In short

UI4AI is a local-first design tool. It collects no personal data. No account, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising. Your work stays on your device.

1. What we collect

Nothing. UI4AI does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information about you. There is no account system, no usage analytics, and no crash reporting that sends data to us.

  • No personal data — we do not ask for your name, email, or any identifier.
  • No tracking — no cookies, no fingerprinting, no third-party analytics.
  • No ads — the app does not display advertising.

2. How the app works

The macOS app embeds a local server that runs entirely on your computer (at 127.0.0.1) to render the editor. All processing happens locally, on your device. The app makes no external network requests.

3. Your designs and files

Your designs are stored only where you save them — on your device, or anywhere you choose. They are never uploaded to us. The web version stores design data in your browser's local storage, which stays on your machine and is never transmitted to us.

4. External links

The app and website may contain links to external services (for example, a purchase page or your AI tool of choice). These services are operated by their respective providers and are governed by their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their practices.

5. Children's privacy

UI4AI is not directed at children, and because we collect no data at all, there is no information from children to be concerned about.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on our GitHub repository.
